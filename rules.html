WHAT IS A DRAFT?

A Draft is a non-official competitive game mode where two clubs of five trainers face each other across six player-selected race tracks. Each trainer submits one Uma, and the clubs themselves shape the matchup through track picks, vetoes, Uma bans, and snake-order selections.

THE DOMINATOR DRAFT 2026

1. Club Composition

The bracket contains 12 clubs. Every club has 5 trainers: one Sprint, one Mile, one Medium, one Long, and one Dirt specialist. Only two clubs compete in a match at a time.

2. Tiebreaker Track

The draft starts by rolling a random tiebreaker track. It is held in reserve and is only raced if the match has not produced a winner through the first six races.

3. Track Draft & Veto

  1. Each club selects 4 tracks from the random track pool.
  2. This creates 8 selected tracks in total.
  3. Each club vetoes 1 track selected by the opposing club.
  4. The final match card contains 6 standard tracks + the reserved tiebreaker track.

4. Uma Pre-Bans

Before selection begins, each club bans 1 Uma it does not want available in the draft. Two Umas are therefore removed from the selection pool before picks begin.

5. Uma Selection

  1. Each club picks 5 Umas in snake order.
  2. After both clubs have five, each club bans 1 Uma from the opposing club's selected batch.
  3. Each club then chooses 2 additional Umas.
  4. That leaves each club with a final pool of 6 playable Umas.
  5. Because a club has five trainers, one of those six Umas is benched.

6. Training & Submission

Once tracks and Umas are locked, the clubs receive 24 hours to train and submit their builds.

Important: the submitted build must run on all selected tracks in the match. You are not submitting separate builds for separate courses.
  • Submit only drafted Umas.
  • The Uma's running style must remain the same across all races.
  • The Uma must have been trained during the 24-hour draft window.
  • Each trainer submits exactly 1 Uma — no more, no less.

The first club to reach 25 points wins the match. If no winner has been decided by the end of the sixth standard race, the clubs proceed to the pre-rolled tiebreaker track.

8. Disqualification / NPC Replacement

Failure to follow the submission rules may result in a player DQ. A DQ'd player is replaced by an NPC Uma that cannot earn points.

If the NPC occupies a scoring position, that position's points pass down to the next eligible Uma. In other words, the first three eligible finishers receive 4 / 2 / 1.
