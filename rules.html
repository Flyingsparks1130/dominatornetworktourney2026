OFFICIAL FORMAT

WHAT IS A DRAFT?

A Draft is a non-official competitive format where two clubs of five players face each other across five drafted race tracks. All five players field one Uma in every race, and the matchup itself is shaped by track picks, vetoes, Uma bans, and snake-order selections.

THE DOMINATOR DRAFT 2026

1. Clubs & Matchups

The tournament contains 12 clubs, with 5 players per club. There are no distance or style specialist roster slots.

Only two clubs compete head-to-head in a normal bracket match. Each club fields all five players, and each player runs one submitted Uma in every standard race.

2. Tiebreaker Track

The draft begins by rolling a random tiebreaker track. It is revealed before the normal track draft and held in reserve.

If a winner has not been decided after the five standard races, the clubs race the pre-rolled tiebreaker.

3. Track Draft & Veto

  1. Each club selects 4 tracks from the random track pool.
  2. This creates 8 selected tracks.
  3. Each club vetoes 1 opposing selection.
  4. The remaining draft pool produces the 5 standard race tracks, with the separately rolled tiebreaker held in reserve.
All five players race on all five standard tracks.

4. Uma Pre-Bans

Each club bans 1 Uma before selection begins.

5. Uma Selection

  1. Each club selects 5 Umas in snake order.
  2. Each club then bans 1 Uma from the opposing batch.
  3. Each club selects 2 additional Umas.
  4. Each side finishes with a 6-Uma playable pool.
  5. One Uma is benched because each club has five players.

6. Training & Submission

After tracks and Umas are finalized, both clubs have 24 hours to train and submit their builds.

The submitted build must be used on every selected track.
  • Submit only drafted Umas.
  • Running style must stay the same across every race.
  • The Uma must have been trained during the 24-hour window.
  • Each player submits exactly 1 Uma.
  • All five submitted Umas are fielded in all five standard races.

The first club to reach 25 points wins. If neither side reaches 25 through the five standard races, the match proceeds to the pre-rolled tiebreaker.

8. DQ / NPC Replacement

A DQ'd player is replaced by an NPC Uma that cannot score. If an NPC occupies a scoring place, the points pass down to the next eligible Uma.

9. Tournament Bracket

Round 1

All 12 clubs are randomly drawn into 6 head-to-head matches. The six winners advance directly and are seeded 1–6.

Redemption

The six Round 1 losers enter a 5-round redemption round robin, fielding 2 Umas each per redemption round. The top two advance as seeds 7 and 8.

8-Club Bracket

  • Seed 1 vs Seed 4
  • Seed 2 vs Seed 3
  • Seed 6 vs Seed 7
  • Seed 5 vs Seed 8
  • Semifinal 1: 1/4 winner vs 2/3 winner
  • Semifinal 2: 6/7 winner vs 5/8 winner
  • Grand Final: SF1 winner vs SF2 winner

The same five-standard-races / first-to-25 / tiebreaker-if-needed format applies at every normal head-to-head bracket stage.
