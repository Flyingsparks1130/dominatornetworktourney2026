OFFICIAL FORMAT

Tournament rules

A Draft is a non-official competitive format where two clubs of five players face each other across six drafted race tracks, with one additional tiebreaker held in reserve. All five players field one Uma in every race, and the matchup itself is shaped by track picks, vetoes, Uma bans, and snake-order selections.

THE DOMINATOR DRAFT 2026

1. Clubs & Matchups

The tournament contains 11 clubs, with 5 players per club. There are no distance or style specialist roster slots.

Only two clubs compete head-to-head in a normal bracket match. Each club fields all five players, and each player runs one submitted Uma in every standard race.

2. Tiebreaker Track

The draft begins by rolling a random tiebreaker track. It is revealed before the normal track draft and held in reserve.

If a winner has not been decided after the six standard races, the clubs race the pre-rolled tiebreaker.

3. Match Track Draft

Each matchup has its own track pool, picks, vetoes and final race card. Open that matchup and select Match draft to see its draft, including the reserved tiebreaker.

All five players race on the six-track card. The match ends early if a club reaches 25 points.

4. Uma Pre-Bans

Each club bans 1 Uma before selection begins.

5. Uma Selection

  1. Each club selects 5 Umas in snake order.
  2. Each club then bans 1 Uma from the opposing batch.
  3. Each club selects 2 additional Umas.
  4. Each side finishes with a 6-Uma playable pool.
  5. One Uma is benched because each club has five players.

6. Training & Submission

After tracks and Umas are finalized, both clubs have 24 hours to train and submit their builds.

The submitted build must be used on every selected track.
  • Submit only drafted Umas.
  • Running style must stay the same across every race.
  • The Uma must have been trained during the 24-hour window.
  • Each player submits exactly 1 Uma.
  • All five submitted Umas are fielded in each race played on the six-track card.

The first club to reach 25 points wins. If neither side reaches 25 through the six standard races, the match proceeds to the pre-rolled tiebreaker.

8. DQ / NPC Replacement

A DQ'd player is replaced by an NPC Uma that cannot score. If an NPC occupies a scoring place, the points pass down to the next eligible Uma.

9. Tournament Bracket

The supplied bracket has 11 clubs and four rounds. Five clubs enter directly in the quarterfinals.

R1 — Round 1

  • Dominion vs Dominarium
  • Domichill vs Domineer
  • Dominium vs Dominante

R2 — Quarterfinals

  • Dominator vs Dominarium
  • Dominance vs Dominant H
  • Dominacion vs Domineer
  • Dominium vs Dominate

R3 — Semifinals

The winners of the first two quarterfinals meet in Semifinal 1. The winners of the last two meet in Semifinal 2. Seeds 1 and 2 occupy opposite halves.

R4 — Grand Final

The semifinal winners play for the championship. The winning club receives five Discord Nitro prizes, one per player.
