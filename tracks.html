PLAYER-CONTROLLED COURSE SELECTION

THE TRACK DRAFT

There is no fixed five-race course list. The matchup itself determines the standard tracks.

01 · RESERVE

Roll tiebreaker

One random track is revealed first and reserved for a seventh race if required.

02 · PICK

4 tracks each

Club A and Club B each select four tracks from the random pool, producing eight picks.

03 · VETO

1 opposing track each

Each club removes one track chosen by its opponent.

04 · LOCK

5 + tiebreaker

The drafted/vetoed pool is reduced to five standard race tracks; the separately rolled tiebreaker remains in reserve.
