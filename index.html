THE MATCH FLOW
01

Roll the tiebreaker

A random tiebreaker track is revealed before anything else.

02

Draft + veto tracks

Each club picks four tracks. Each then vetoes one opposing pick, leaving six race tracks.

03

Ban + draft Umas

Pre-bans, snake-order picks, second bans, then two additional selections per club.

04

Train, submit, race

24 hours to train. One submitted Uma per trainer, one drafted Uma benched, same build across every selected track.
